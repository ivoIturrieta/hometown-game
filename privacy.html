Hometown

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Hometown is a city-building game made by Ivo Iturrieta. It has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking across apps. The only thing it sends about you is anonymous gameplay stats, and you can switch those off.

What stays on your iPhone. Your saved games, the towns you build and your settings are kept on your iPhone, and they are deleted when you delete the app. The only things that leave it are the stats and the map requests described below.

What Hometown sends. To learn which towns are fun and where the game is too hard, Hometown sends anonymous stats to PostHog, an analytics service. For example: which ready-made town and which start you chose (in the game, a town you built yourself is called "custom"), what you build, the streets you open, how floods, fires and quakes ended, years and elections, money choices, the cards you close, the screens you visit, and errors (a page error, or a town that could not open). Each event also says the language the game is in, whether the music is on, the text size and whether Reduce Motion is on, the app version, how much memory the device has (rounded), and a random app ID that isn't linked to you. Hometown doesn't store your IP address's location, doesn't record your screen, and doesn't collect your name, email, contacts, photos or your device's location.

Turning the stats off. On Hometown's first screen, switch Anonymous stats off. From then on Hometown sends nothing, and it remembers your choice.

Building your own town. When you search for a place, the search runs on your iPhone with Apple Maps. The stats include what you typed in the search (its first 40 letters), whether a place was found, and the place you picked (its name, region and country), so we learn which towns people want and which ones can't be found. If you search for the town you live in, that tells us roughly where you live, though never who you are; switch Anonymous stats off if you'd rather not send it. To build it, Hometown downloads open data about that area: map tiles from OpenFreeMap, heights from the Terrain Tiles on Amazon Web Services, ten years of weather from NASA POWER and earthquake records from the U.S. Geological Survey. These requests carry the area's coordinates, not who you are; like any website, those services see your IP address.

Purchases. Any purchase in Hometown is made through Apple, and Hometown never sees your payment details. The stats note that a purchase was offered, made or restored, never who you are.

Children. Hometown doesn't ask for or collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
