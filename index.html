Hometown

Be the mayor of a real town, cut from paper: its streets, rivers, hills and dangers come from real maps and real weather.

Getting started

Pick one of the ready-made towns, or tap Your town and type any town in the world: Hometown builds it on your iPhone from open map data. Start as a village of sixteen homes, as a town, or as the city today. Open streets and families come; build what they need; keep your districts' promises, because they vote every four years.

Questions

How do I start a town again? Tap the town's name at the top, then choose a start under "Start … again". Tap it a second time to confirm.

How do I go to another town? Tap the town's name, then Other towns. Your game there is kept.

A message is in the way. Every card has a ✕ in its corner, and the bottom card also closes with a swipe down. During a flood, fire or quake a red alert stays at the top; tap it to see the disaster again.

What is free? Every town is free for its first term (four years) when you start it as a village or a town. One purchase, Keep governing, unlocks every year after, in every town, and starting a town as the city today. No ads, no subscription. On a new iPhone, tap the town's name, then Keep governing, then Restore purchase.

Can I turn the music off? Tap the town's name, then Music. Game sounds stay on.

Which languages? English, Japanese, Spanish, German, French and Portuguese (Brazil). Hometown follows your iPhone's language; you can also choose one for Hometown alone in Settings › Apps › Hometown.

Building my town says it needs the internet. Ready-made towns play offline; building a new one downloads its map, heights and weather, so it needs a connection for a minute or two.

Anonymous stats. You can switch them off on the first screen. See the privacy policy.
